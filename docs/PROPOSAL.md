# Agency Proposal (Step 0): ASA Enrolment Agency (AI + Chinese)

**Student:** Oleg Mishchenko · **Course:** Programming for Data Science (RNU) · **Lecturer:** Amit Joshi

**Agency type:** Education / Tutoring Operations Agency (own proposal, not one of the five examples).

**Client and problem.** International School Premjers in Riga runs after-school activities (ASA). Two new
programs start this year: an AI Club for children aged 7 to 15 and Chinese language classes (Beginner to HSK3).
Today registration, grouping, parent emails, reminders, parent questions and reporting are done by hand by one
teacher. That is slow, error prone (duplicates, missing consent, timetable clashes for children in both programs)
and gives the school no live numbers.

**Services (5).**
1. Application intake from the registration form, one form for both programs.
2. Data quality and consent checks.
3. Clash-free group scheduling by program, age band or Chinese level, and availability.
4. Parent communications: confirmations, waitlist notices, lesson reminders, FAQ replies.
5. Reporting: live dashboards, daily brief to the teacher, promotional posts and poster copy.

**Why 7+1 autonomous employees fit.** Each service is a separate, repeatable job with clear inputs and outputs,
and the jobs form a pipeline (intake, QA, scheduling, communications) plus three parallel roles (support,
content, analytics). A Project Manager is needed to dispatch work, track every task in a Google Sheet ledger,
hold anything that leaves the school (emails, posts) until the Boss approves, and answer the Boss's questions
from real data.
